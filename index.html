David Sehnal, Ph.D.

I build software for working with molecular structures. The best known is Mol*, the 3D viewer used by RCSB PDB, PDBe and AlphaFold DB, where I'm the lead architect. I also built the data formats and services behind it (BinaryCIF, ModelServer, VolumeServer) and the newer MolViewSpec and MolViewStories.

On the industry side, I spent five years at Iambic Therapeutics, going from frontend engineer to principal software engineer and building the platform that connected their AI models to the lab. Currently at a stealth startup. I also lead a small research group at Masaryk University in Brno.
